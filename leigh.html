Why This Matters

LGBTQ+ visibility can literally change lives. Seeing someone like Leigh live openly can:

  • Inspire: Give hope to those who feel alone or misunderstood.
  • Normalize: Show that queer identities are a natural, beautiful part of human diversity.
  • Empower: Encourage others to set boundaries, seek safe spaces, and honor their own identity.

This fan page exists to say: Leigh, and everyone like Leigh, deserves love, respect, and joy—exactly as they are.

How Fans Can Show Support

  • Create safe spaces: Online and offline, make it clear that queer people are welcome and valued.
